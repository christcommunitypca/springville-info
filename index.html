Springville's Charlie Hare signs with Gadsden State

Published November 17, 2026

Springville High School senior Charlie Hare signed to play baseball for Gadsden State, with one more season ahead for the Tigers.

Springville High School center fielder Charlie Hare signed his letter of intent on Nov. 12 to play college baseball for the Gadsden State Cardinals, according to a Nov. 17 report in The Trussville Tribune.

Hare's junior season was cut short by injury. Before then he was batting .375 with a .460 on-base percentage. He plans to return for one more Tigers season, expected to begin in March.

New Springville art gallery planned for Highway 11

Published November 11, 2026

Dibble Dabble Art Gallery announced a Nov. 12 opening at 6441 U.S. Highway 11, with art by local and central Alabama artists.

Dibble Dabble Art Gallery announced a grand opening and ribbon cutting for Nov. 12 at 6441 U.S. Highway 11 in Springville, according to a Nov. 11 report by The Trussville Tribune.

The opening-day schedule included a glassblowing event and an evening open house. The gallery was described as a place to see work by local and central Alabama artists. Check with the gallery directly for current visiting hours.
