Springville Has St. Clair County's Only Cast-Iron Storefront

Did you know the Presley Store at 601 Main Street has the only cast-iron storefront in St. Clair County? Built in 1902 for brothers Porter and John Presley, it's a one-of-a-kind part of downtown Springville.

Summer Readers Grow More Than 3,200%

When Jamie Twente arrived in 2015, Springville Public Library's previous summer reading record was just 25. In 2026, participation reached 829—more than 3,200% above that earlier mark. A remarkable achievement for Twente and the library team.
